Distortion Club is back! Here is the programme of our night-time club events ?

Distortion is a celebration of Copenhagen’s underground and nightlife. Once again, we’re teaming up with our city’s beloved clubs and venues to deliver a top-tier programme of international headliners and local legends.

Distortion Club is spread across Module, Rust, Den Anden Side, Culture Box, Pumpehuset, Klub Werkstatt and Søpavillionen. Events take place from Wednesday 3 June to Friday 5 June 2026. Free entry to ALL Club events with a Distortion Festival Pass (link in bio)

Please note that for some Club events, the age limit is 21+ and due to limited capacity, access is on a first-come, first-served basis. You will need to follow each club’s own entrance and house rules.
